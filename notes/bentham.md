Bentham and the Panopticon

I'm so excited to share Bentham with you all
- An Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation was the first piece of political theory I ever loved because it is proof of what taking things too far gets you.
- Jeremy Bentham opens that book with the memorable line: "Nature has placed mankind under the governance of two sovereign masters, pain and pleasure. It is for them alone to point out what we ought to do, as well as to determine what we shall do...The principle of utility recognizes this subjection, and assumes it for the foundation of that system, the object of which is to rear the fabric of felicity by the hands of reason and of law."
  - and with those words he lays the foundation for "utilitarianism" which is, one of the most influential theories of all history. Utilitarian thinking is the "effective altruism" that motivated Bill Gates to donate mosquito nets in africa (peter singer) 
  - okay so what it is? what is the princinple of utility? 
  - all the basic idea that we should seek to promote happiness and weigh decisions based on their tendency to promote happiness and diminish pain.
  - Bentham is not the first person to have this idea, but he is the first person to systemize it and he's super influential on J.S. Mill who goes on to write _On Liberty_. Bentham actually apparently met a young J.S. Mills and then wrote a letter to a mutual friend being like "what a weirdo."
